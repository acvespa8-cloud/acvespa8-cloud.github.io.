Research Pack privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

Research Pack is a personal research tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers. No one else can sign up for or use it.

Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Research Pack uses two Google Drive permissions:

  • drive.file: to create and manage the folders and files it makes itself. These are one private folder per research run, holding a download checklist, the finished reading packet and recovery checkpoints.
  • drive.readonly: to read the articles the owner deliberately saves into a run's Uploads folder. Research Pack limits itself in code to that folder and to the checkpoints it created. It does not list, open or read anything else in Drive.

How the data is used

  • Files are used only to prepare the owner's own research packets.
  • Research Pack never creates sharing links or changes sharing permissions.
  • It never moves, edits or deletes the owner's files.
  • Text from articles the owner submits may be processed by AI assistants the owner subscribes to, to help prepare the reading guide. This happens on the owner's instruction, and only for that run's uploads.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

  • Research files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computers.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computers, in a private location readable only by the owner's account. It is never copied into research files or published.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Research Pack's files in Drive are ordinary files the owner can delete.

Research Pack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
