Research Pack

A personal research tool, not a public service.

Research Pack turns one research question into an organized packet of original sources and a reading guide for its owner's own reading. It runs on the owner's own computers.

Research Pack is used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else and accepts no sign-ups.

It uses Google Drive only to keep the owner's own research files in the owner's own Drive. How it handles that data is described in the privacy policy.
